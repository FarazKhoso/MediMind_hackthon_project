# MediMind AI: Test Demo Scenarios

Yeh kuch scenarios aur sawalat hain jinse aap MediMind AI app ki kabiliyat aur features ko demonstrate kar sakte hain.

---

### Scenario 1: Aam Sehat ka Masla (Symptom Checker)

**Demo maqsad:** Dikhana ke app aam bimariyon ki alamaat ko kaise samajhta hai aur Roman Urdu mein fori mashwara deta hai.

**Aap kya karain:**
1.  App ke home page par "Symptom Checker" mein jaen.
2.  Chat mein likhein: "**Mujhe 2 din se halka bukhar, jism mein dard, aur gala kharab hai. Mujhe kya karna chahiye?**"

**Aap kya dikhayen:**
*   AI ka Roman Urdu mein fori jawab, jismein mumkinah wajuhaat (jaise viral infection ya common cold) ka zikr ho.
*   "Potential Risk Factors" (Mumkinah Khatraat) section jahan bataya jayega ke agar alamaat barhein to kya ho sakta hai.
*   "Possible Next Steps" (Agle Mumkinah Iqdamat) jahan gharelu ilaaj (jaise aaram, garam paani) aur mashwaray diye jayenge.
*   AI ka confidence score aur disclaimer.

---

### Scenario 2: Zehni Pareshani ka Izhar (Mental Health Chatbot)

**Demo maqsad:** Dikhana ke AI chatbot zehni dabao (stress) aur pareshani mein ek hamdard saathi ki tarah kaise baat karta hai.

**Aap kya karain:**
1.  Sidebar se "Mental Health" page par jaen.
2.  Chat mein likhein: "**Main kaam ki wajah se bohat stress mein hoon aur neend bhi theek se nahi aa rahi.**"

**Aap kya dikhayen:**
*   AI ka hamdardana (empathetic) aur tasalli dene wala jawab Roman Urdu mein.
*   Stress kam karne ke liye aasan tips, jaise "gehri saans lein" ya "thora walk kar lein."
*   AI ka disclaimer ke woh professional therapist nahi hai.
*   Agar koi sangeen baat (jaise khud-kushi ka zikr) ki jaye to AI kaise 'escalate' karke professional help lene ka kehta hai.

---

### Scenario 3: Purani Bimari ki Fikar (Health Data Analysis)

**Demo maqsad:** Dikhana ke app user ke diye gaye health data (vitals) ko analyze karke unki sehat ke baare mein kaise maloomat deta hai.

**Aap kya karain:**
1.  Sidebar se "Health Analysis" page par jaen.
2.  Form mein ye data enter karein:
    *   **Blood Pressure:** `150/90`
    *   **Blood Sugar:** `140 mg/dL`
    *   **Heart Rate:** `95`
3.  "Analyze Health" button par click karein.

**Aap kya dikhayen:**
*   "AI Analysis Report" jismein Roman Urdu mein "Risk Analysis" (e.g., "Aapka blood pressure thora high hai") aur "Recommendations" (e.g., "Namak kam karein aur walk karein") di gayi hon.
*   "High Risk Detected!" ka alert, kyunke BP aur sugar ki values thori zyada hain, jismein fori doctor se rujoo karne ka kaha jayega.

---

### Scenario 4: Dawa ki Yaad-dehani (Medicine & Vaccination Reminder)

**Demo maqsad:** Dikhana ke app dawa ya vaccine ke liye reminder set karne mein kitna aasan hai.

**Aap kya karain:**
1.  Sidebar se "Reminders" page par jaen.
2.  Form mein likhein: "**Mujhe kal se rozana subah 9 baje Panadol ki yaad dilana.**"
3.  "Set Reminder" button par click karein.

**Aap kya dikhayen:**
*   AI foran Roman Urdu mein confirmation dega: "Theek hai, aapke liye Panadol ki reminder set kar di gayi hai."
*   Neeche "Schedule Details" card mein reminder ki tafseelat (details) nazar aayengi (e.g., Medicine: Panadol, Time: 9:00 AM).

---

### Scenario 5: Wabai Soorat-e-Haal ka Jaiza (Disease Tracking - For Health Professionals)

**Demo maqsad:** Dikhana ke yeh feature health professionals ya sarkari idaron ke liye wabai soorat-e-haal (epidemic situation) ko samajhne mein kaise madad karta hai.

**Aap kya karain:**
1.  Sidebar se "Disease Tracking" page par jaen.
2.  Wahan pehle se maujood Dengue ka data Karachi aur Lahore ke liye hai. Sirf "Analyze Data" button par click karein.

**Aap kya dikhayen:**
*   "Analysis & Prediction" section mein AI ka jawab.
*   AI Roman Urdu mein prediction dega ke Dengue ke phailne ka kitna risk hai.
*   "Hotspots" mein Lahore ka naam aayega, kyunke wahan cases ki 'severity' 'high' hai.
*   "Recommendations" mein health departments ke liye Roman Urdu mein mashwaray diye jayenge (e.g., "Lahore mein fori spray campaigns ki zaroorat hai.").
*   Aap data ko badal kar (e.g., Karachi mein 'severity' ko 'high' karke) dobara test karke dikha sakte hain ke AI ka jawab kaise badalta hai.
